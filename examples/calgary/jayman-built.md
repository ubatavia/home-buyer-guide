## Overview
Large Calgary and Edmonton builder. Its Glacier Ridge model pages state prices **before GST**, for home and lot together. The builder reports its front-attached garage homes in Glacier Ridge are in their "final opportunities", so availability of the Karma 24 and Holly 24 needs checking with the sales centre.

## Pricing & Included Features
Glacier Ridge starting prices, read directly from the builder's Glacier Ridge model pages on 2026-10-19 (pre-GST, home and lot):

| Plan | Size | Starting price | Incl. 5% GST |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brooklyn 20 | 1,450 sq ft | $520,000s | about $546,000 |
| Karma 24 | 2,261 sq ft | $750,000s | about $787,500 |
| Holly 24 | 2,436 sq ft | $770,000s | about $808,500 |

The same models show lower starting prices on Jayman's Calgary-wide model pages (for example Karma 24 from the $630,000s), because those pages report the lowest price across all communities. The community-specific figures above are the ones that matter here. An earlier search snippet of Jayman's Glacier Ridge community page showed "$660,000s including GST"; direct reading of the model pages contradicts it, so it was dropped.

The builder states that single-family homes include $31,000 of energy-efficient upgrades at no cost (builder claim).

## Matching Floorplans
**Karma 24** (front attached, 2-car): 2,261 sq ft, 3 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms. Oversized kitchen island and walk-through pantry, with a spice-kitchen option and a main-floor bedroom option. The closest match in size to Cedarglen's Windsor (2,238 sq ft).

**Holly 24**: 2,436 sq ft, 3 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, 2-car garage. Oversized owner's suite with a private flex area; the main floor has a flex room or can add a main-floor bedroom with its own ensuite. Gallery shows a bonus room and a developed lower level. Garage orientation is not stated on the page.

**Brooklyn 20** (laned): 1,450 sq ft (1,444 on the Calgary-wide page), 3 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms. Resale and under-construction listings describe the layout: open kitchen, dining and great room with a powder room and rear mudroom on the main floor; three bedrooms, full bath, laundry and a primary suite with walk-in closet and ensuite upstairs; an unfinished lower level with a side entrance and a bathroom rough-in; a detached double garage.

Size-based comparison with Cedarglen: `floorplan-comparison.md`. Floor plan drawings are on the builder's pages (not copied here): the Karma, Holly and Brooklyn model pages linked in the sources.

## Build Quality & Trades
- Warranty runs through the mandatory, independent Alberta New Home Warranty Program (builder's own site), with two scheduled service calls in the first year (about 2 and 11 months after possession).
- No superintendent-specific evidence found (`unknown`).
- Trade quality is `mixed`: one Calgary reviewer (AvidRatings, October 2020) said repairs from the two-month service visit were still outstanding four months after moving in and felt trades prioritized speed. An Edmonton 2010 build owner (HomeStars) rated the builder 5/10 after repeated furnace, roof and plumbing issues over five years.

## Reputation / Reviews / Social Signals
- AvidRatings, Edmonton, 56 reviews: home quality 4.4, time to correct items 3.6, would recommend 4.6. This is the Edmonton operation, not Calgary, so it is a proxy.
- Awards: the builder reports a 2024 Calgary H.O.M.E. Award for Best Customer Experience (self-reported, a weaker signal).
- Reddit threads were searched and none surfaced.
- Overall: strong on quality and recommendation, weaker on how quickly deficiencies are fixed. The sample is small and partly dated.
