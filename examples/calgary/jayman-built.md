## Overview
Large Calgary and Edmonton builder. In Glacier Ridge its page lists 26 homes (per search snippet of the page) with starting prices from the $660,000s to the $690,000s, stated to include home, lot and GST. Those are laned homes with a detached rear garage. The builder reports its front-attached garage homes here are in their "final opportunities", so the remaining new stock is laned.

## Pricing & Included Features
Brooklyn 20 listed from the $660,000s; Edward 18 from the $675,000s. The page has no date. The $825,000 upper bound comes from resale listings of 2026 show homes, not the builder's price list, so it is indicative only. Price per square foot on the Brooklyn 20 at an assumed $665,000 and about 1,450 sq ft is roughly $459.

## Matching Floorplans
**Brooklyn 20** is the best-documented plan. Across resale and under-construction listings it is consistently described as about 1,438 to 1,466 sq ft, three bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms:
- Main floor: open kitchen, dining and great room, powder room, rear mudroom
- Upper floor: three bedrooms, full bath, laundry, primary with walk-in closet and ensuite
- Lower level: unfinished, with side entrance and a rough-in for a third bathroom
- Detached double garage (one listing: 22' x 20'), 9' ceilings on the main floor

Floor plan drawings are on the builder's model pages (not copied here, as they are the builder's artwork): https://jayman.com/find-your-home/communities/glacier-ridge/ (Home Models tab). Listing examples: https://wahi.com/ca/en/real-estate/ab/calgary/57-ambleton-blvd-nw-calgary-t3p2l3-alberta

## Build Quality & Trades
- Warranty runs through the mandatory, independent Alberta New Home Warranty Program (builder's own site), with two scheduled service calls in the first year (about 2 and 11 months after possession).
- No superintendent-specific evidence found (`unknown`).
- Trade quality is `mixed`: one Calgary reviewer (AvidRatings, October 2020) said repairs from the two-month service visit were still outstanding four months after moving in and felt trades prioritized speed. An Edmonton 2010 build owner (HomeStars) rated the builder 5/10 after repeated furnace, roof and plumbing issues over five years.

## Reputation / Reviews / Social Signals
- AvidRatings, Edmonton, 56 reviews: home quality 4.4, time to correct items 3.6, would recommend 4.6. This is the Edmonton operation, not Calgary, so it is a proxy.
- Awards: the builder reports a 2024 Calgary H.O.M.E. Award for Best Customer Experience (self-reported, a weaker signal).
- Reddit threads were searched and none surfaced.
- Overall: strong on quality and recommendation, weaker on how quickly deficiencies are fixed. The sample is small and partly dated.
