## Which one matches the Holly?

You suspected the Holly lines up with the Carbon or the Windsor. On the published numbers:

- **Windsor is the closest match.** Same 3 bedrooms and 2.5 baths, a front double garage, and the nearest size (2,238 vs 2,436 sq ft, about 8% smaller). Both have a bonus or flex space upstairs.
- **Carbon is the match if you want flexibility.** At its base size it is smaller than the Holly (2,032 to 2,260 sq ft), but it scales to 5 bedrooms, so a configured Carbon can match or beat the Holly's use of space.
- Holly 24 is the largest of the three. None of this is exact: layouts differ, and builders count square footage differently.

## Source conflicts worth knowing

- Jayman's Glacier Ridge page says its starting prices include GST; the Calgary model pages say "Pre-GST". They may differ by date or product. Ask the sales centre.
- The Brooklyn 20 shows "from the $490,000s" on its model page but "from the $660,000s" on the Glacier Ridge page, because the model page reports the lowest price across all communities.
