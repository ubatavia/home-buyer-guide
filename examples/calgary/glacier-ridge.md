## Overview & Location
An 832-acre master-planned community in northwest Calgary, developed by Anthem, framed by foothills views and natural coulees (Jayman community page). Cedarglen describes it as about 25 minutes from downtown; that is a builder claim, not independently measured. Developers' pages list the nearby Sage Hill Quarter shopping centre at about four minutes and the Beacon Hill centre (Costco) at about six minutes south on Sarcee Trail.

## Schools (detail)
- Jayman reports four elementary school sites and one high school site in the plan. Reserved sites are not schools.
- The Calgary Board of Education's 2027-2030 capital plan, approved 2026-03-20, includes Glacier Ridge: a Glacier Ridge Middle (grades 6 to 9, 885 permanent capacity, about $46M) and a high school at the planning stage. These are **requests to the province**, which decides funding and timing. The CBE document labels the middle school's year inconsistently with its news release, so check the full plan.
- Catholic board plans: not checked, so unknown.
- Until schools open, children attend schools in neighbouring communities. Which school an address is zoned to must be confirmed with the school boards.

## Parks & Amenities
About 10 km of trails and pathways. The Residents' Association facility, "Glacier Ridge Village", had its grand opening in April 2026 per an Anthem press release (gymnasium, event spaces, outdoor recreation areas).

## Future Development / Risks
- Schools are the main risk: requested, unfunded, and years away. Families with young children should plan around bussing to neighbouring communities.
- The community is still under construction, so expect construction traffic and noise.
- Jayman says it is selling its "final opportunities" for front-attached garage homes here, so the community's mix is shifting toward laned and paired homes.
- The Residents' Association is described by Anthem's materials as a compulsory structure maintaining amenities. The fee amount was not found.
