## Overview & Location
Master-planned community in northwest Calgary developed by Anthem. Cedarglen describes it as about 25 minutes from downtown (builder claim, not independently measured). Nearby retail named by builders includes Walmart, Costco and T&T.

## Schools (detail)
Jayman's page lists four elementary school sites and one high school site; Anthem refers to five future school sites. Existing schools serving residents are in neighbouring communities such as Nolan Hill and Sage Hill (per a neighbourhood guide). Which school a given address is zoned to must be confirmed with the school boards. This is why `schools` is not a confident list.

## Parks & Amenities
Over 10 km of trails and parks per the builder. A Residents' Association building (gym, skating ribbon, toboggan hill) was planned to open in 2026, so confirm it opened and what it costs.

## Future Development / Risks
- School sites are reserved, not built. Timing is the City's and school boards' decision.
- The community is still under construction, so expect construction noise and traffic for years.
- Price spread is wide because paired, laned and front-drive homes are all sold here.
