# Calgary example: Glacier Ridge

A worked example of the workflow, built from **public** information gathered on 2026-10-19.

- One community: **Glacier Ridge** (northwest Calgary)
- Two builders: **Jayman BUILT** and **Cedarglen Homes**
- An **illustrative household**. The income, savings and contract rate in `scenario.md` are assumptions for the demo, not anyone's real finances.

Every figure carries a source and retrieval date. Where the evidence is thin the file says `null` or `unknown` and sets `data_confidence: low`. Coverage:
- Community: location, amenities, school-board capital plan, Residents' Association status
- Floor plans: Jayman Holly 24 and Brooklyn 20, Cedarglen Windsor and Carbon, compared by size in `floorplan-comparison.md` (links to the builders' drawings, not copies of them)
- Residents' Association fee: $498.02 per year (2026), from the association's own FAQ
- Builders: warranty program, review evidence from HomeStars, AvidRatings and TrustBurn

Prices are builder "starting from" figures from marketing pages and search snippets. No show-home visit or quote backs them, so they are not first-hand. Cedarglen's site blocks automated reading, so its price bands come from search snippets. Review samples are small and partly from Edmonton or from several years ago. Confirm current prices and details with the builders before relying on anything here.

Not available from the sources found (marked unknown in the files): deposit schedules, upgrade pricing, transit service, Catholic school plans, superintendent-level quality, and Glacier Ridge-specific Holly, Windsor and Carbon prices.
